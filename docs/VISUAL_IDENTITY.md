# ILogic visual identity — preserve when refining

The owner's original screenshots are the visual authority: near-black surfaces, soft olive/lime illumination, matte organic ribbons, subdued planetary shapes and metallic coils, outlined lime emphasis, generous display typography, pill actions and fine rules. Do not replace these with flat generic card layouts or a light palette.

Original artwork remains untouched. The 13 optimized WebP copies in `src/assets/img/identity/` total approximately 523 KB and preserve those compositions. `IdentityBackdrop` applies the original layers to hero, story, services, work, process and closing CTA. Decorative images have empty alt attributes and do not obscure content.

Selected projects use one large central canvas with rotated whole-text side rails on desktop. Mobile removes the side rails and keeps explicit project-selection buttons. Real project screenshots still require the supplied files; placeholder frames are deliberate and must not be replaced with unrelated template projects.

The process uses large circular icon orbits, a fine extending arc and a lime focus state. All four supplied steps and their descriptions remain visible. The closing CTA restores the original background and angled dark pills on desktop; mobile uses straight, readable actions.

Before the process, a continuous strip repeats the four ILogic phrases in alternating white and lime outline: understand the idea, work out the logic, build the solution and improve the result. Bright lime ILogic monograms separate the phrases in the scrolling strips; decorative monograms elsewhere remain matte. It has a pause control and a static, fully readable reduced-motion variant. The hero's text social links use the same centralized configuration as all other social links and remain in a single row, with lime hover and keyboard-focus states.

The four existing trust statements below the hero use the same reusable marquee: since 2023, live projects in the Russian market, a multidisciplinary team, and serving Egypt and the Gulf. Their wording stays unchanged. This longer copy scrolls more slowly and keeps its own pause control; neither strip's control affects the other.

The partners page uses smaller copies of the same original artwork, while the rest of the site retains its existing copies. New pictograms and the hero diagram use native SVG with currentColor. The OG artwork is authored as SVG and exported mechanically to WebP, with no people or invented product screenshots. The same font families are now self-hosted as WOFF2 with their licenses. Loading only the shared styles on the partners page and deferring complete legacy styles must preserve their original order and appearance on other routes.
