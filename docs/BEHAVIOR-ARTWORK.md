## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: portfolio cover illustration for ILogic's user behavior analytics project. Primary request: communicate a clear visual story that analyzing what users click, browse and show interest in helps a business identify and reach the right customers. Premium cinematic photorealistic 3D software visualization, landscape 3:2, composed to remain understandable at small website card size. On a matte charcoal desk in a seamless near-black studio, a slim dark glass analytics display forms the central focal point. On its left side, a short lime cursor trail links three large abstract product/content tiles, representing observed user actions. The trail enters a simple understated analysis/segmentation node at the center and exits as three elegant customer profile cards on the right: abstract anonymous head-and-shoulder line symbols only, with one restrained lime interest marker each; one small message-outline icon at the final selected profile signals reaching a relevant customer. One continuous fine lime line makes this journey easy to follow, not a complicated network. Materials: smoked glass, black anodized metal, shallow embossed interface geometry, subtle specular studio highlights, carefully controlled depth and soft shadows. ILogic palette: near-black #101010, charcoal #191b16, muted olive, restrained lime #ccff00 as the only saturated accent. Strong hierarchy and generous negative space, front three-quarter view with all conceptual elements easily visible. No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no charts suggesting verified growth, no prices, no ratings, no logos, no real banking brand, no currency, no facial photographs, no real people, no glowing AI brain, no robots, no arrows exploding out of the screen, no sci-fi neon overload, no watermarks.
